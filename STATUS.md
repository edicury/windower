## Notes for next session
- **Live testing of Phase 19 got as far as the sidecar layer and stopped at the daemon.** Verified working against the real binary: `describe` advertising `input.mouse`/`input.keyboard`/`screenshot`; `captureFrame` (320x208 PNG at maxWidth 320, aspect + downscale correct); `performInput` moving the cursor, with the pixels→points conversion proven correct on a scale-2 display (sent 1500,980 px → cursor landed at 750,490 pt); `INPUT_OUT_OF_BOUNDS` rejecting an off-screen coordinate. One trap worth knowing: the sidecar exits on stdin EOF, and a posted `CGEvent` can be lost if the process dies before delivery — keep the process alive (e.g. a trailing `sleep`) when smoke-testing `performInput` by piping a single line, or it looks like a silent no-op.
- Three `operate` runs failed at the daemon, not the operator: `PERMISSION_DENIED` (daemon spawned by a shell without Screen Recording) and `OPERATOR_MISSING_API_KEY` twice (daemon spawned by a shell without the key). The daemon inherits both the environment and the TCC identity of whichever process first spawns it, and freezes them. **This is what Phase 20 exists to fix.** Until it lands, the workaround is: `windower daemon stop`, then run from a shell that has both the API key exported and the TCC grants.
